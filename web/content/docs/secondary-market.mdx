# Secondary market

Cards trade on Sherhood Trade (fixed-price USDG) and can also appear on OpenSea. A mystery card and a revealed card are **different products**.

## Mystery vs revealed

| State | What you buy | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Unrevealed** | A lottery ticket on a basket | Ownership % and rarity unknown until reveal |
| **Revealed** | A priced claim | Known weight × basket value; can claim stock tokens |

Every listing shows **Unrevealed** or **Revealed**.

## $SHRH boost on trade

Boost requires escrowing enough `$SHRH` via `lockLuck` at **deposit** (sets `luckLocked` on the card) **and** still having that escrow at **reveal** (current holder). You cannot unlock in the same block you lock — that blocks same-tx flash loans. Mystery cards stay tradeable; the reveal-time escrow check follows whoever holds the card.

## Listing lock

While a card is listed on Trade, the NFT **cannot** be transferred, claimed, or early-exited. Cancel the listing first. Claiming **burns** the card — a redeemed card no longer exists, so worthless shells can never be traded here or on OpenSea.

## Royalty

Trade takes **2.5%** to the protocol. OpenSea may display the same rate; enforcement off-app is optional per venue.
