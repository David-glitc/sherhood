# Buying and selling cards

List once in the app. The order is **Seaport** (OpenSea). It shows on OpenSea and on Sherhood Market.

## Sealed and revealed

| State | What you know |
|-------|----------------|
| **Sealed** | Deposit, basket, estimated value |
| **Revealed** | Ownership %, rarity, holdings |

## List

1. Open [Collection](/inventory) or a Sherd page.
2. Enter an **ETH** price.
3. Tap **List** — wallet signs a Seaport order.
4. The listing appears on OpenSea and [/marketplace](/marketplace).

## Buy

From Market or a Sherd page, **Buy** opens the OpenSea item to fulfill the same order.

## Cancel

Manage or cancel on [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/collection/sherds) for that token.
