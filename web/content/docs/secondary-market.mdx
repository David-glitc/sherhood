# Buying and selling cards

Sherhood Trade lets you buy and sell cards for a fixed USDG price. Check the card state, basket, ownership, and value before you confirm.

## Sealed and revealed cards

| Card state | What you know |
|------------|---------------|
| **Sealed** | Deposit, basket, and current estimated value |
| **Revealed** | Ownership percentage, rarity, basket holdings, and claim preview |

A sealed card carries more uncertainty because its final ownership percentage is still hidden.

## List a card

1. Open [Cards](/inventory).
2. Choose a card that is not claimed or already listed.
3. Enter the USDG price.
4. Select **List on Trade**.
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Your card stays in your wallet, but it is locked while the listing is active.

## Buy a card

1. Open [Trade](/marketplace).
2. Review the card state and basket.
3. Compare the asking price with the displayed card value.
4. Confirm **Buy card**.
5. Approve the USDG payment if your wallet asks.

The card transfers after the purchase confirms.

## Cancel a listing

Open your listing on Trade and select **Cancel listing**. After cancellation confirms, you can transfer, claim, or exit the card again.

## Listing restrictions

A listed card cannot be:

- Transferred to another wallet.
- Claimed.
- Exited.
- Listed again.

Cancel the listing first.

## Fees

Sherhood Trade charges a 2.5% royalty on the sale price. The seller receives the amount left after the royalty.

## Claimed cards

Claiming removes the card permanently. It disappears from Sherhood Trade and cannot be sold again.
