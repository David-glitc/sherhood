# Allocation

After a basket buys its stock portfolio, Sherhood assigns every card a positive ownership weight.

## Guarantees

```
Σ ownership = 100% of the basket
every card ownership > 0
```

Expected ownership tracks deposit size. Luck adds variance around fair share — it does not eject losers.

## How reveal works

1. Funding is closed; deposits are fixed. The basket already holds its stocks.
2. For each card with deposit `d`, luck draws a multiplier:

```
m ∈ [0.50×, 2.00×]
raw = d × m
```

3. Normalize across the basket so weights sum to 100%.
4. Floor repair: every card gets at least a tiny share; the sum stays exactly 100%.
5. Rarity follows the multiplier band:

| Multiplier | Rarity |
|------------|--------|
| 0.50 – 0.90 | Common |
| 0.90 – 1.20 | Rare |
| 1.20 – 1.60 | Epic |
| 1.60 – 2.00 | Legendary |

## $SHRH luck boost

When `$SHRH` is live, holding enough of it can boost your reveal multiplier (capped at the usual ceiling). Default boost: **+25%**.

**Dual check (lock + reveal):**

1. At **deposit** (when you lock into the basket), your wallet must already hold at least the threshold — that moment is recorded on the card.
2. At **reveal**, the wallet that holds the card must still hold at least the threshold.

Both must pass. Threshold is set by the protocol after `$SHRH` launches — target is the amount of `$SHRH` equal to about **0.055 ETH** at launch price.

Stock **purchase** luck (which tickers get bought) is separate from this reveal boost.

## Early exits and 100%

If a card exits during funding, it is burned before reveal. Allocation only runs over remaining cards, so ownership still sums to exactly 100%. See [Early exit](/docs/early-exit).

## Expected value

Larger deposits still imply higher expected ownership. For a card depositing `d` into total `D`:

```
E[ownership] ≈ d / D
```

Rough relative outcomes vs fair share in a large equal-deposit basket:

| Extremes | About |
|----------|-------|
| Soft floor | ~0.4× fair share |
| Soft ceiling | ~1.6× fair share |
