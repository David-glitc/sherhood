# Reveal and rarity

Reveal turns a sealed card into a readable ownership claim. You’ll see the basket’s stocks, your ownership percentage, and the card’s rarity.

## Before reveal

A sealed card shows your deposit and basket, but its final ownership percentage is hidden.

Your deposit still matters. Larger deposits generally receive a larger expected share, while reveal adds variation between cards.

## After reveal

Your card shows:

- **Ownership:** the percentage of the basket assigned to the card.
- **Rarity:** Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary.
- **Stocks in the basket:** the stock tokens held by the basket.
- **Claim preview:** the token amounts available if you claim.

All revealed cards together represent the full basket. Every active card receives a positive share.

## What rarity means

Rarity describes how the card performed at reveal relative to its deposit.

| Rarity | What it signals |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Common** | A share near the lower end of the reveal range |
| **Rare** | A share around the middle of the range |
| **Epic** | A share above the middle of the range |
| **Legendary** | A share near the upper end of the range |

Rarity does not guarantee a market price. Check the ownership percentage and basket value before buying or listing a card.

## $SHRH luck

The $SHRH luck feature is not active until the token launches.

When active, the app will show:

- The required $SHRH amount.
- When the balance must be held.
- Whether the card qualifies.
- The available luck boost.

Do not buy $SHRH from an address that is not published in the Sherhood app and official channels.

## Cards that leave before reveal

A card that exits during funding is removed permanently and does not participate in reveal. The remaining active cards share the basket.
