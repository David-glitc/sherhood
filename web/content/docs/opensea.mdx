# OpenSea

Sherhood cards can appear on OpenSea once the collection is submitted. Treat OpenSea as a satellite venue — [Trade](/marketplace) on sherhood.xyz remains the primary market because it shows derived value and claim/exit next to the listing.

## Metadata

Each card’s metadata updates when it reveals (name, art, traits). Sealed cards show Unrevealed state; revealed cards show rarity, ownership weight, and constituents. After reveal, refresh the token on OpenSea so listings do not keep sealed art.

**Claiming burns the card.** A redeemed card is removed from the collection entirely — you can never buy a worthless already-claimed shell on OpenSea. If a token disappears from a listing, its owner claimed or early-exited.

## Royalties

Secondary sales carry a **2.5%** royalty to the protocol via the on-chain ERC-2981 standard, which OpenSea reads and applies at checkout. Royalties on Trade (sherhood.xyz) are enforced by the contract itself; on external venues they follow that venue's creator-fee policy. Prefer Trade when you want claim/exit next to the sale.

## Filters that matter

`State`, `Rarity`, `Basket`, `Ownership Weight`, `Constituents`, `Deposit (USDG)`.

## Link

Once the collection is live, OpenSea appears in the app nav. Until then, use Trade on sherhood.xyz.
