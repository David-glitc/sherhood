# OpenSea

Sherhood cards can appear on OpenSea after the collection goes live. Use Sherhood Trade when you want basket value, claim actions, and listing status in one place.

## Find the official collection

Use the OpenSea link in the Sherhood navigation. Do not trust collection links from direct messages or search ads.

Before buying, check:

- The collection link came from sherhood.xyz.
- The card belongs to the basket shown in its details.
- The card still exists and has not been claimed or exited.
- The wallet and network in OpenSea are correct.

## Card details

A sealed card shows its unrevealed artwork and basket information.

A revealed card can show:

- Rarity.
- Ownership percentage.
- Basket.
- Stocks in the basket.
- Original deposit.

OpenSea may cache older card details. Use its metadata refresh action if a revealed card still shows sealed artwork.

## Buying on OpenSea

OpenSea checkout does not show all Sherhood basket information.

Before buying:

1. Copy the card ID.
2. Find the same card in Sherhood.
3. Review its basket, state, ownership, and displayed value.
4. Return to OpenSea and confirm the final price and fees.

## Royalties

The collection requests a 2.5% creator royalty. External marketplaces decide how creator fees are applied at checkout.

Sherhood Trade applies its royalty directly and shows the card’s basket context beside the listing.

## Removed cards

Claiming or exiting removes the card permanently. It should disappear from marketplace listings.
