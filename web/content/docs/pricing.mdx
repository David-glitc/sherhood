# Understanding card value

The app shows a value estimate beside each card. Use it as a reference when you list or buy. It is not a guaranteed sale price.

## Sealed cards

Before reveal, the estimate uses:

- The card’s deposit.
- The basket’s total deposits.
- The latest basket value available to the app.

The final ownership percentage can be higher or lower after reveal.

## Revealed cards

After reveal, the estimate uses:

- The card’s ownership percentage.
- The stock tokens held by the basket.
- The latest available prices for those tokens.

This gives you a clearer view of the assets behind the card.

## Why the estimate can change

Card value can move when:

- Stock token prices change.
- The basket’s displayed price data updates.
- A sealed card reveals a different ownership percentage.
- A marketplace listing includes a premium or discount.

Market prices may be delayed or unavailable. Check the basket holdings and current prices before making a decision.

## Compare a listing

Before buying:

1. Check whether the card is sealed or revealed.
2. Open the basket and review its holdings.
3. Compare the listing price with the displayed estimate.
4. Review the royalty and network fee.
5. Decide whether the uncertainty or premium fits your risk.

Sellers can choose any asking price. Sherhood does not force a minimum or maximum listing price.

## Claimed cards

A claimed card has no remaining basket assets. Sherhood removes it permanently after claim so it cannot be listed again.
