# Fees

Sherhood earns from a few explicit take rates. Creators do **not** configure protocol fees in the create form.

## Create fee

When you deploy a **community basket**, you pay a flat **create fee** in USDG once.

- Charged on [Create](/create)
- Sent to the protocol treasury
- Shown in the UI before you confirm

Platform baskets created by Sherhood may skip this fee.

## On deposits

| Fee | Who sets it | Typical default |
|-----|-------------|-----------------|
| Protocol fee | Protocol (bps of deposits, taken before the stock buy) | ~1% |
| Entry fee | Protocol / pot config (flat per deposit) | often 0 |
| EntryRouter fee | Protocol (bps when entering with ETH / WETH) | ~0.5% |

ETH / WETH entries swap to USDG on Uniswap; the router fee is skimmed from swap output, then the net USDG is deposited into the basket.

## Early exit

While a basket is still funding, `earlyExit` burns the mystery card and refunds **95%** of the deposit in USDG. The remaining **5%** is an exit fee to the treasury. Entry fees already paid stay with the pot.

Later windows (post-close haircut, post-reveal inventory spread) are specified in [Early exit](/docs/early-exit) and not all live yet.

## On secondary trades

[Trade](/marketplace) charges a **royalty** on sale price (default **2.5%**) paid to the treasury. The card contract also exposes the same **2.5% via EIP-2981** for OpenSea and aggregators. Cards themselves are minted by funding baskets — Trade is optional secondary only.

Derived value (deposit share or ownership weight × basket NAV) is documented in [Pricing](/docs/pricing) — use it next to ask price when judging a trade.

## Example on $100k primary volume

| Stream | Rough take |
|--------|------------|
| Protocol 1% | ~$1,000 |
| EntryRouter 0.5% (if all enter via ETH/WETH) | $0–$500 |
| Early exit 5% on $10k exited deposits | ~$500 |
| Marketplace 2.5% on $20k secondary | ~$500 |

Exact numbers depend on live fee params after deploy.

## What you keep

Your card’s claim is on the **net** portfolio after protocol fees are pulled at purchase. Reveal still divides 100% of that remaining pool among card holders — claim pays out a slice of each holding.
