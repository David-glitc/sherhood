# Fees

Review the amount shown in the app and your wallet before every transaction. Network fees and Sherhood fees are separate.

## At a glance

| Action | Fee |
|--------|-----|
| Fund with USDG | Basket fees shown before confirmation |
| Fund with ETH or WETH | Basket fees, conversion fee, and network fee |
| Create a community basket | One-time USDG creation fee |
| Exit during funding | 5% of the refundable deposit |
| Buy or sell on Trade | 2.5% royalty on the sale price |
| Claim a revealed card | Network fee |

Live values shown in the app take priority over examples in this guide.

## Network fee

Every wallet transaction requires ETH for Robinhood Chain gas. Your wallet shows the estimated network fee before you confirm.

## Funding with USDG

USDG enters the basket directly. The basket page shows any Sherhood fee before you fund.

## Funding with ETH or WETH

ETH and WETH are converted to USDG before the deposit reaches the basket.

Your final amount depends on:

- The current ETH or WETH price.
- Your selected slippage limit.
- The conversion fee.
- Any basket fees.

If the conversion would return less than your minimum, the transaction is cancelled instead of depositing at a worse rate.

## Creating a basket

Community basket creators pay the USDG creation fee shown on the [Create](/create) page. You pay it once when the basket is created.

## Exiting during funding

An early exit refunds 95% of the eligible deposit in USDG. The other 5% is the exit fee.

Fees paid when you originally entered are not refunded.

## Trading a card

Sherhood Trade charges a 2.5% royalty on the sale price. The buyer pays the listed USDG amount, and the seller receives the amount left after the royalty.

Other marketplaces may apply creator fees differently. Review their checkout before confirming.
