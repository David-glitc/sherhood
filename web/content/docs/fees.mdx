# Fees

Review the amount shown in the app and your wallet before every transaction. Network fees and Sherhood fees are separate.

## At a glance

| Action | Fee | Goes to |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Create a community pool | **$5 USDG** (waived for deployer / $SHRH holders when live) | Protocol fee wallet |
| Fund (card / entry) | **$0.50 USDG** per deposit on new pools | Protocol fee wallet |
| Fund with ETH / WETH | **0.5%** conversion fee on USDG out, plus entry fee | Protocol fee wallet |
| Protocol cut at purchase | **1%** of pool deposits (factory default) | Protocol fee wallet |
| Exit during funding | **5%** of refundable deposit | Protocol fee wallet |
| Buy / sell on Trade | **2.5%** royalty on sale price | Protocol fee wallet |
| Claim a revealed Sherd | Network fee only | — |

Live values in the app take priority if a pool was created with different immutable params.

## Where fees go

All protocol USDG fees are forwarded on-chain through the **Treasury** contract straight to the **fee wallet**:

`0xC24F7118f55d0643a82A1594cbCbb7484011a251`

That wallet receives create fees, entry fees, ETH/WETH router fees, early-exit fees, protocol bps at purchase, and Trade royalties.

**Lifetime protocol fees** (all-time USDG forwarded) show on the [Pools](/app) stats row as **Lifetime fees**.

OpenSea and other external markets may apply their own creator-fee rules; on-chain Trade always uses the 2.5% path above.

## Network fee

Every wallet transaction needs ETH for Robinhood Chain gas. Your wallet shows the estimate before you confirm.

## Funding with USDG

USDG enters the pool directly. The pool page shows any Sherhood fee before you fund.

## Funding with ETH or WETH

ETH and WETH convert to USDG before the deposit. Your final amount depends on price, slippage, the **0.5%** conversion fee, and any entry fee. If conversion would land below your minimum, the tx reverts.

## Creating a pool

Community creators pay the **$5 USDG** creation fee shown on [Create](/create). **$SHRH holders** can create fee-free when the waiver is live — see [$SHRH](/docs/shrh).

## Exiting during funding

An early exit refunds **95%** of the eligible deposit. The other **5%** is the exit fee. Entry fees paid when you funded are not refunded.

## Trading a Sherd

Sherhood Trade takes **2.5%** of the sale price. The buyer pays the ask; the seller receives the rest after royalty.
