# Positions and claims

After reveal, your Sherd is a **position** in the pool’s stock tokens. Claiming is optional — you can hold indefinitely, trade the card, or claim when you want the underlying tokens in your wallet.

## Interaction rules

| Stage | Allowed | Not allowed |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Funding** | Fund, early exit (−5%), trade sealed card | Claim stock tokens |
| **Closed / Purchased** | Hold, trade | Early exit, claim |
| **Revealed** | Hold, trade, claim anytime | Early exit, refund |
| **Cancelled** | Full deposit refund | Claim, trade as a live position |

## Position growth

While your Sherd is revealed and **unclaimed**:

- You own a fixed **ownership %** of the pool’s stock holdings.
- The **mark** of that position moves with live stock prices (same quotes used on profile / inventory).
- Claiming converts that share into ERC-20 stock tokens in your wallet and burns the Sherd.
- Holding does not earn extra ownership — growth is price mark, not dilution.

## Tight rules (product)

1. **No forced claim** — dust sweeps wait until every participant has claimed.
2. **One claim per Sherd** — claim burns the card; it cannot be listed again.
3. **Trade before claim** — listed cards must be delisted (or sold) before claim.
4. **Early exit only while Funding** — after close, wait for reveal (or cancel/refund if the pool never filled).
5. **Cancel path** — if the deadline passes under goal and the pool is cancelled, refund recovers your deposit (entry fee stays with the protocol).

## Why hold unclaimed?

- Keep a tradable Sherd on OpenSea or Sherhood Trade.
- Track mark / day move on your profile without claiming.
- Claim later when you want the tokens in-wallet.
