# Getting started

This guide shows you how to fund a basket, receive a sealed card, and claim or trade it after reveal.

## Quick start

1. Open [Baskets](/app) and connect your wallet.
2. Choose an open basket.
3. Select USDG, ETH, or WETH and enter an amount.
4. Review the minimum deposit and slippage setting.
5. Confirm **Fund basket** in your wallet.

Your card appears in [Cards](/inventory) after the transaction confirms.

## Before you fund

You need:

- A wallet connected to Robinhood Chain.
- ETH for network fees.
- USDG, ETH, or WETH for the deposit.

Sherhood is experimental software. Only fund an amount you can afford to lose.

## Choose a basket

Each basket shows:

- Its funding goal and current progress.
- The minimum deposit.
- When funding closes.
- How many cards have been created.

Open the basket details page before you deposit. Check the basket address shown in the app and confirm that it is still accepting funds.

## Choose how to pay

You can fund with:

- **USDG:** deposited directly into the basket.
- **ETH:** converted to USDG before it enters the basket.
- **WETH:** converted to USDG before it enters the basket.

For ETH and WETH, choose the maximum slippage you accept. A lower setting gives you more protection but may cause the transaction to fail during fast price changes.

## Follow your card

Your card starts sealed. It shows the basket and your deposit, but not your final ownership percentage.

When the basket closes:

1. The basket buys 2 to 5 stock tokens.
2. Your card reveals its rarity and ownership percentage.
3. The card shows the stock tokens behind your claim.

Open [Cards](/inventory) to follow its status.

## Claim or trade

After reveal, you can:

- **Claim assets** to receive your share of every stock token held by the basket.
- **List on Trade** to sell the card for a fixed USDG price.

Claiming permanently removes the card because its assets have been redeemed.
