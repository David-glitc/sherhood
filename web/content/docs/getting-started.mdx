# Getting started

Sherhood is a fractional stock-basket protocol on Robinhood Chain. You fund a **basket**, mint a **mystery card**, and after the basket buys a multi-stock portfolio, reveal sets how large your ownership slice is.

Nobody is wiped to zero. Claim **size** is random; every card still owns something.

## 1. Connect a wallet

1. Open [sherhood.xyz](/app).
2. Click **Connect** and pick your wallet.
3. Switch to **Robinhood Chain** if your wallet asks.

Gas is paid in **ETH**. Settlement into baskets uses **USDG** (or ETH / WETH auto-swapped).

## 2. Fund a basket

1. Go to **[Baskets](/app)**.
2. Pick an open basket.
3. Choose pay-in: **ETH**, **WETH**, or **USDG**.
4. Enter an amount at or above the minimum, then **Fund**.

You receive a **mystery card** immediately. Ownership % stays hidden until reveal. Stocks are picked when the basket closes — not when you join.

## 3. Wait for close and purchase

When the funding goal is hit (or the duration ends with deposits), the basket closes and luck-picks **2–5** stock tokens. Your card stays sealed until reveal.

## 4. Reveal

After purchase, reveal runs. Each card gets an **ownership weight** and a **rarity**. All weights sum to **100%**.

Optional: hold enough **$SHRH** at deposit **and** at reveal for a luck boost (see [Allocation](/docs/allocation)). `$SHRH` launches soon.

## 5. Claim or trade

- **Claim** from [Cards](/inventory) to withdraw your share of every stock in the basket.
- Or **list** the card on [Trade](/marketplace).

## Create your own basket

On [Create](/create) you set goal, min deposit, and duration. You do **not** pick which stocks the basket will buy. Community baskets pay a small create fee in USDG once.
