# $SHERD

**$SHERD** is the Sherhood token on Robinhood Chain.

**Official contract:** [`0xe429dbb6b55532685C7eAE41DbF052934449aCc1`](https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com/token/0xe429dbb6b55532685C7eAE41DbF052934449aCc1)

Treat any other address as fake.

## What it does

Holding $SHERD unlocks product perks as they go live:

- **Luck at reveal** — Hold $SHERD when you fund and when the pool reveals to qualify for the luck boost on that Sherd.
- **Create for free** — Hold $SHERD to waive the community pool creation fee when the waiver is active.
- **More later** — Roadmap items may add further holder benefits; the app shows each perk when it ships.

You only need to **hold** $SHERD in the connected wallet at the moments the product checks. The app shows whether you qualify.

## How to get $SHERD

1. Connect a Robinhood Chain wallet.
2. Tap **Buy $SHERD** → Flap or Uniswap.
3. Hold in that wallet for perks.

You can also fund pools with $SHERD (swaps to ETH at mark when needed).

Never send funds to a contract you found in a DM or random link.

## Safety

- Only use the contract address shown above and in the Sherhood app.
- Sherhood never DMs you a “claim” link for $SHERD.
- Holding $SHERD does not guarantee profit, rarity, or pool outcomes — luck is a boost, not a fixed payout.
