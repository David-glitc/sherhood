# XP & streaks

Sherhood tracks **XP** for on-chain actions and rewards **streaks** when you show up on consecutive days.

## How you earn

| Action | XP |
|--------|-----|
| Fund a basket (deposit) | 100 |
| Create a community basket | 250 |
| Card reveals | 50 |
| Claim stock share | 150 |
| Buy a card on Trade | 75 |
| Sell a card on Trade | 75 |
| Early exit (funding) | 25 |

## Streaks

Any scoring action on a calendar day counts toward your streak. Each consecutive day adds a **+10% XP multiplier** on that day’s gains, capped at **+50%** (5-day streak).

Miss a day and the streak resets.

## Leaderboard

Open [Leaderboard](/leaderboard) to see top wallets by total XP, current streak, and recent actions.

XP is derived from on-chain activity — it is a progress layer, not a transferable token.
