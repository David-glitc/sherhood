# XP and streaks

XP records your activity across baskets, cards, and Trade. It is a score, not a token, and it cannot be transferred.

## XP actions

| Action | XP |
|--------|----|
| Fund a basket | 100 |
| Create a community basket | 250 |
| Reveal a card | 50 |
| Claim stock tokens | 150 |
| Buy a card on Trade | 75 |
| Sell a card on Trade | 75 |
| Exit during funding | 25 |

Self-trades (same wallet as buyer and seller) earn **no** XP.

The [Leaderboard](/leaderboard) shows total XP, current streak, rank, and action count by wallet.

## Daily streaks (UTC)

Complete at least one XP action during a **UTC calendar day** to keep your streak active.

Consecutive active days increase the XP earned that day:

| Streak | Daily bonus |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 day | No bonus |
| 2 days | +10% |
| 3 days | +20% |
| 4 days | +30% |
| 5 days or more | +50% |

If you miss a UTC day, the **current streak resets to 0**. Past day bonuses already earned stay in your total XP.

## When XP appears

XP updates after the related transaction is confirmed and indexed. The leaderboard may take a short time to refresh.

If a score does not update:

1. Confirm the transaction succeeded in your wallet.
2. Wait for the leaderboard to refresh.
3. Reload the page.

## What XP does not do

XP does not:

- Represent money or ownership.
- Change a card’s reveal.
- Reduce fees.
- Transfer between wallets.
