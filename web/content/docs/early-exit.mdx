# Early exit

You can leave a basket before claiming — payout depends on **when** you exit.

| Window | Payout | Adjustment | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-close** (funding open) | Deposit refund | Flat **5%** exit fee | Burn mystery card; refund 95% USDG |
| **Unmet goal after deadline** | Full deposit | **0%** fee | Call `cancel`, then `refund` per card |
| **Post-close / post-reveal** | — | — | Not live — claim or Trade instead |

## Live today

- **Early exit** while funding: burn card, 5% fee, 95% USDG refund.
- **Cancel + refund** if the deadline passes without hitting the funding goal (full principal).
- **Listing lock:** a card listed on Trade cannot be transferred, claimed, or exited until you cancel the listing.

## Not live

- Post-close / pre-reveal haircut exit
- Post-reveal instant NAV exit

See also [Fees](/docs/fees) and [Pricing](/docs/pricing).
