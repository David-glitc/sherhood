# Exits and refunds

You can exit while a basket is still funding. If a basket misses its goal, you can request a full refund after cancellation.

## Exit during funding

An early exit:

- Permanently removes the sealed card.
- Returns 95% of the eligible deposit in USDG.
- Charges a 5% exit fee.
- Keeps previously paid entry fees.

The app shows the expected refund before you confirm.

## How to exit

1. Open the basket details page.
2. Find **Your cards**.
3. Choose the card you want to exit.
4. Review the refund and fee.
5. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Wait for confirmation before trying another action with the same card.

## Refund after an unmet goal

If the basket reaches its deadline without meeting the required goal:

1. The basket becomes eligible for cancellation.
2. Open the basket details page.
3. Request the refund for each card you own.

This refund returns the eligible deposit without the 5% early-exit fee.

## When exit is unavailable

You cannot exit a card when:

- The basket is no longer in the funding stage.
- The card is listed for sale.
- The card was already removed, claimed, or exited.
- A recent exit is still inside the one-hour wait period.

Cancel an active listing before trying to exit.

## After the basket closes

Instant post-close exits are not available. Wait for reveal, then claim the stock tokens or list the card on Trade.
