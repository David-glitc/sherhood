# Protocol

Sherhood turns pooled money into a multi-stock portfolio. Each joiner gets an NFT card that represents their claim.

## Mental model

> Everyone keeps a claim. Luck decides **how large** that claim is — not whether you exist.
>
> A second luck path picks **which stocks** the closed basket buys. Creators do not set the ticker list when they open a basket.

Cards mint when you deposit. Reveal only assigns weight and rarity.

## Lifecycle

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|----------------|
| **Create** | Platform or community opens a basket (goal, min, duration) |
| **Funding** | Users deposit ETH / WETH / USDG; each deposit mints a mystery card |
| **Close** | Goal met or duration expires (with deposits) |
| **Purchase** | The basket luck-picks 2–5 stock tokens and buys them |
| **Reveal** | Luck assigns ownership weights that sum to 100% |
| **Claim / Trade** | Redeem a share of every holding, or sell the card |

## Basket rules

- One basket buys a **portfolio** of 2–5 registered stock tokens when it fills.
- Creators pick goal, min deposit, and duration — not tickers or fee rates.
- Concurrent baskets can run in parallel.
- Claiming redeems a proportional slice of **all** holdings in that basket.

## What Sherhood is not

- Not a winner-take-all raffle as the core product.
- Not a bet where chance can wipe your deposit to zero.
- Not off-chain custodial ownership without an on-chain claim NFT.

## Flow

```
Connect wallet
    → Fund basket (ETH / WETH / USDG)
    → Mystery card minted
    → Basket closes & luck-buys 2–5 stocks
    → Reveal ownership %
    → Claim portfolio share  or  Trade card
```
